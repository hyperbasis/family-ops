# Optional AI prompts

These are examples. They do not install a Skill, connect accounts, or create an autonomous agent.

## Triage

"Summarize the information I provide and suggest whether it updates an existing Case, creates a task, belongs in Reference, or needs no further action. Separate verified facts from assumptions."

## Status review

"Using only the records I supply, tell me what this is, what action comes next, whether a reliable deadline exists, and what remains unverified."

## Handoff

"Summarize all pending Cases, Tasks and Deadlines in the records provided, noting waiting states and unresolved questions. Do not assume actions were completed unless confirmed."

# Getting started

Family Ops is a way of working, not a service you must install. Start with an **entirely fictional or nonsensitive matter**.

## Option A: Without AI

1. Create a **private** folder or storage area you control. You can copy the material in [template](template/README.md) there.
2. Open the ledger examples with any spreadsheet program and keep separate views of Cases, Tasks, Deadlines, and Reference.
3. Put the original document (if there is one) in an appropriate location and record its source. Do not substitute your own summary for the original.
4. Record what the matter is, its current status, the next action, the person responsible, and any verified deadline.
5. Update the same record when new information arrives. Close the matter only after you have actually verified its outcome.

## Option B: With AI assistance

Follow Option A first. Then optionally let a trusted AI assistant read only the information you deliberately provide or authorize it to access. Start with [example prompts](template/AI_PROMPTS.md). Ask it to identify likely related records and propose updates, but review its proposals before they become official household state.

Reading email, editing a calendar, and sending a message are separate actions with separate permissions. Check actual execution results. Never assume an AI can connect to an account just because a prompt mentions it.

## What to copy and what not to copy

The files in this repository are **examples**, not your private household database. GitHub's “Use this template” feature copies tracked files and folders; it does not set up your accounts, storage, or automatic agents.

Keep actual household data in **separately controlled private storage**, ideally outside any publicly shared repository. Do not replace the fictional records here with real family records and then push the repository publicly.

See [Privacy and safety](README.md#privacy-and-safety).
